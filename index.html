Coverage gaps and limitations

    How records are built

    Each record is created from at least one source that was opened during the scan. Fields marked Sourced in the detail panel repeat what the source says; fields marked Analyst judgment are the research team's assessment and can be wrong. Missing information is shown as "Not publicly disclosed" rather than estimated. A republished article is not treated as a new development: the history timeline only gains an entry when a stage, award, value, party or date changes.

    Priority score (0 to 20)

    Five criteria, each scored 0 to 4. High priority is 15 or more, Medium 10 to 14, Low below 10. Completed, cancelled and suspended projects are not scored and are shown as Context.

    Opportunity types

    • Confirmed procurement activity: a tender, EOI, PPP qualification or award stage is documented in a source. This is not the same as an open consultancy tender; the detail panel says which roles are actually procurable.
    • Potential future opportunity: inferred from scope and stage; no procurement notice has been seen.
    • Existing engagement or reference: SJ Group already holds a role; tracked for account management.
    • Track only: closed delivery, completed, cancelled, suspended, or high-risk. Retained for context and excluded from active totals.

    Region assignment

    Each country belongs to exactly one region. Türkiye is placed in Europe. Georgia, Armenia and Azerbaijan are grouped with Central Asia. Egypt, Libya, Tunisia, Algeria and Morocco form North Africa. Australia is its own region; New Zealand and the Pacific sit in Asia Pacific. North America covers the United States, Canada and Mexico (added 12 Sept 2026 at the BD team's request; Central America and the Caribbean are not yet covered). Change the mapping in the data files if the BD team uses a different split; totals will follow.

    Dates and freshness

    Scan time is when the sources were last checked (shown in the header). Latest update on a record is the date of its most recent material development according to the sources, which is usually earlier. Records whose latest update is older than 180 days are flagged stale automatically. Conflicting figures between sources are kept and flagged rather than resolved silently.

    SJ Group capability basis

    Capabilities were checked against SJ Group's own site (aviation listed under Building and Cities; project management consultancy at King Abdulaziz International Airport, Jeddah), Passenger Terminal Today's April 2018 report of Surbana Jurong Consultants' appointment as Master Building Consultant and Master Civil Consultant for Changi Terminal 5, and SMEC's statement on detailed design consultancy for Noida International Airport with Robert Bird Group and SJ Aviation. Service suggestions in each record are the analyst's mapping of scope to these capabilities, not confirmed SJ intent.
